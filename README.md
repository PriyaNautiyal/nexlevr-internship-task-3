Nykaa – Social Listening Analysis

About

A social listening project focused on tracking and analysing customer mentions of Nykaa across social media platforms and online reviews over a one-week period.

Objectives

* Monitor brand mentions and customer feedback.
* Categorise mentions into Praise, Complaints, Questions and Ideas.
* Analyse customer sentiment.
* Summarise findings using a spreadsheet.
 Outcome

Organised customer feedback into four categories to understand brand perception and identify areas for improvement.
